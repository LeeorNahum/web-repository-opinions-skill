# Marrow Journal

Marrow Journal is a private daily journal on the web. It is sold on privacy: entries are visible only to the person who wrote them.

## Decided

- No sharing, no teams, no collaborators, now or later. This is the product's positioning, not a missing feature.
- Sign-in through a managed auth provider with social sign-in and email.
- Paid plan by subscription, one subscriber per account.
- The signed-in app has: today's entry, a calendar of past entries, an entry page, search, and settings.

Nothing is built yet.
