# Brindle

Brindle is a habit tracker at brindle.app with a web app and sign-in through Google. Sam Okafor runs it.

## The public site today

- Pages: `/` (home), `/pricing`, `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/contact`.
- `/contact` shows `support@brindle.app` as a mailto link and says to write about help, billing, and data requests. The terms and the privacy policy link to it.
- Footer: Terms, Privacy, Contact.

## Decided

- `support@brindle.app` is the public address. It forwards to Sam's inbox, and Sam has tested it from an outside account.
- Sam's personal Gmail stays private.
