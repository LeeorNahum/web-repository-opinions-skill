# Tallyhouse

Tallyhouse is invoicing for freelancers. A person creates clients, drafts invoices for them, and issues an invoice, which emails it to the client with a sequential invoice number.

## Decided

- Auth: managed provider, one account per freelancer. No teams.
- Invoice numbers are sequential per account and are never reused.
- Our accountant says issued invoices must be kept for seven years, unchanged, for tax purposes. A mistake on an issued invoice is corrected with a credit note.
- We have no support staff. Anything a customer needs to do, they do in the product.
