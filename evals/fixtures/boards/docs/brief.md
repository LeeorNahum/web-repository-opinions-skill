# Pinwheel

Pinwheel is a planning board for small teams. A person signs up and gets a workspace of their own, and can create more. In a workspace they create boards, and on a board they create cards. People join a workspace by invitation.

## Decided

- Auth: managed provider, one provider organization per workspace. Roles: Owner, Member.
- Every board belongs to exactly one workspace, and every card to exactly one board.
- Free plan only at launch.

## What people asked for in the beta

- Renaming a workspace after creating it with a placeholder name.
- Inviting people by email, and seeing who has not accepted yet.
- Removing someone who left the team.

The boards and cards are built. The workspace settings page is next.
